## By course

### Course 1 — Context Engineering & Effective Prompting

Spine: **Designing Context-Aware AI Systems** and **Structured Prompt Engineering** (every unit).
The learner can give AI the background, role, intent, examples and format a job needs, ground it
in real documents, and keep it all in a reusable brief.

### Course 2 — Agentic Automation Design

Spine: **Task Decomposition**, **Agent Design**, and **AI Literacy** (four units each).
The learner can turn a recurring job into an agent spec (aim, trigger, explicit steps, approval
points, limits), build it with free tools, and judge whether a job deserves an agent at all.

### Course 3 — Responsible AI Governance

Spine: **AI Literacy** and **Responsible AI Use & Compliance** (every unit).
The learner can trace claims to sources, test for bias, classify data before sharing it, disclose
AI involvement, and put a named human behind decisions about people.
