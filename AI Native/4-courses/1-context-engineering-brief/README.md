# Course 1 — Context Engineering

*Course capability: Context Engineering & Effective Prompting · Framework: **BRIEF** · 21 sub-units*

**Maya entering:** can't get good output. **Maya leaving:** the output sounds like her.

The same AI gives Maya a flat, generic answer and gives her colleague Ravi a useful one. The course
works out why, one failure at a time: missing facts, a missing voice, a missing purpose, an endless
correction loop, an answer the AI made up, and a chat that forgets everything by Monday. Each fix
becomes one letter of a framework the learner only sees named in Unit 5.

**What the learner leaves with:** a reusable one-page brief, filled once per recurring job and
pasted into any fresh chat. *"Fill it once. Paste it forever."*

| Letter | Meaning | Earned in |
|---|---|---|
| **B** — Background | The facts of your world, and grounding in real documents | Units 1 & 4 |
| **R** — Role | Who the AI should be — the voice | Unit 2 |
| **I** — Intent | The outcome you want, and who's reading | Unit 2 |
| **E** — Examples | Show your voice, don't describe it | Unit 3 |
| **F** — Format | The shape — length, structure, must & must-not | Unit 3 |
