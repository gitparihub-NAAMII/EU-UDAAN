# Course 2 — Agentic AI

*Course capability: Agentic Automation Design · Framework: **AGENT** · 16 sub-units*

**Maya entering:** gets good output, but is exhausted. **Maya leaving:** Monday runs without her.

Course 1 worked, so everything that needs writing now lands on Maya's desk. *"You fixed the writing.
So why are you MORE tired?"* She has become the middleware between her tools. The course moves her
from asking AI for text to handing it a job: what the job is for, what starts it, the exact steps,
where a human must approve, and what it must never touch.

**What the learner leaves with:** an agent spec and a working agent, built free as a Google Gemini
"Gem" step by step alongside Maya. The course closes on judgment: knowing when *not* to build one.
