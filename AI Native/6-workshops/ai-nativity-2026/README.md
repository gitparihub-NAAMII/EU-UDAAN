## Facilitation notes taken from the deck

- **Part 3 runs on "change one thing at a time".** Each round adds exactly one dial in a new chat.
  The "what actually came back" slides (54, 56, 58, 60, 62) show real Gemini output. Re-run them
  before delivering, because the model's output will drift.
- **The refund conflict (one lakh twenty vs one lakh forty) is what links Parts 3, 4 and 5.**
  Limits flags it in round 5, the Writer drafts around it, the Checker holds it. If a change to the
  transcript breaks this conflict, the whole arc breaks.
- **The Shrestha draft is the Checker's second catch** (a Gurung remark leaking into another
  supplier's message). D2-03 records that in testing it did **not** fire, and all three other
  drafts came back SEND. Slides 97 and 98 now both hedge ("It may pass"). If it doesn't
  appear, make D2-03's point instead: the Checker still had to look.
- **Part 4 runs on Gemini skills, not Gems.** Google is retiring Gems: on 2026-11-17 they move
  into Settings, and they stop working no sooner than 2027-03-01 (business) or 2027-06-01
  (education), when leftovers become draft skills. Skills keep everything Part 4 teaches and add
  two things the slides use:
  - **A description field.** It is the only part Gemini reads when deciding whether a skill fits a
    request. Automatic pick-up is best-effort, so participants always call a worker by name
    (`/listener`) in a new chat.
  - **The worker is a file.** A skill downloads as a `.zip` holding a `SKILL.md`. That makes the
    take-home portable.
- **Before you deliver Part 4:**
  - **Re-run the chain.** v4's tested results (the landlord HOLD, the Shrestha non-catch) were
    recorded with Gems. The instructions are the same, except that the Writer and the Checker now point at "the
    file attached to this skill" (the Checker gets one added line). Re-run the chain with skills, and update slides 93, 98, 99 and 100
    and D2-03 if the outputs differ.
  - **Check that skills show for a personal account.** Open Customize → Skills on a fresh personal
    account; the Gemini app rollout was due to finish mid-November 2026. Participants need to be
    18+. Workspace or school accounts may have skills turned off by an admin.
  - **Know why D2-02 is uploaded from the computer.** Participants attach D2-02 from their
    computer, not from Drive. With Keep Activity off, skills can't read Google Drive, and Drive
    files aren't included when a skill is downloaded.
  - **Have the fallback ready.** If someone falls behind, they upload a folder from `final/handouts/skills/`
    (Skills → Create → Upload), then add D2-02 during the upload.
- **The fix-and-recheck loop (slides 99–100) is untested.** Expect round 2 to rewrite the landlord
  message so it asks the landlord to confirm the agreed amount. If the Checker passes that, ask
  the room whether the rewrite fixed the problem or only the wording: the refund is still
  unreconciled until someone checks the chalan. If it still holds, the Checker writes HAND TO A
  HUMAN. Both outcomes make the slide's point. Log the result in `final/testing/README.md`.
- **Part 5 has no exercise on purpose.** Slide 107: you practise it in real decisions, not in a
  sandbox.
