CAO = Chief Administrative Officer (प्रमुख प्रशासकीय अधिकृत). Contacts are exactly as published on each local level's website on 20 Nov 2026. CAOs are transferred often and the websites show no update date, so call to confirm before relying on a name. Some CAOs publish a personal Gmail or Yahoo address; it is listed as published.

Local levels and wards follow the 2017 (2073 BS) restructuring. Ward counts are for planning; check them against MoFAGA / Survey Department boundary data before final mapping. Ticks in the “Mapped” column are saved only in your own browser.
