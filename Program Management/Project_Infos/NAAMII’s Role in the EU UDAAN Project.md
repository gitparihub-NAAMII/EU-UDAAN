## NAAMII’s Role in the EU UDAAN Project: Commitments, Standards and Duties

Note: “M4” means month 4 of the 48-month action, not a calendar month.

### 1. What NAAMII leads

| Commitment | What was promised | When |
|---|---|---|
| AI School (Activity 1.1.1) | Three levels delivered on Tangible Careers, which has to work mainly on phones and offline.<br>**Native:** 20,000 youth, 8 hours of AI literacy and ethics, also shared via Shikshya.org.<br>**Foundation:** 1,500 learners, 60 hours of machine learning and data science, project-based, using Nepal-specific datasets, aimed at fintech, agri-tech and health-tech jobs.<br>**Deep:** 500 advanced learners in deep learning, language AI (NLP) and computer vision, with research projects, GPU computing access and mentors. | Course design M3–5, delivery M4–M42 |
| Internships & fellowships (Activity 2.3.1) | 800 private-sector placements through Tangible Careers: 400 internships of up to 3 months and 500 fellowships of up to 5 months, in a mix of in-person and online. Each person works on real projects with an industry mentor and is assessed on performance. The focus is on paths to long-term jobs. | Outreach and selection M4–6, placements M5–M42 |
| Tangible Careers upgrades | Offline sync, mobile optimisation, Nepal-specific datasets and culturally adapted interfaces. The application describes NAAMII as leading Karmashala “by enhancing the Tangible Careers platform”. | Through the action |

### 2. Standards NAAMII must deliver to

| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Women and inclusion | At least 40% women, priority for marginalised groups, and competitive selection for the advanced level and for fellowships. |
| Placement matching | Based on verified skills, not academic certificates. |
| Teaching approach | A mix of in-person and online delivery, content adapted to Nepal, short repeated sessions, and offline kits as the backup when connectivity fails. |
| Guiding principles | A human-rights-based approach (all five principles) and gender equality and social inclusion. The application also promises to track environmental impact with a “Greening Matrix”. |
| Data | Results broken down by sex, age, disability, rural/urban location and ethnic group. Achievement must be backed by competency rubrics, project portfolios and certificates. |
| Required equipment | High-performance computers, licensed software, reliable internet, and GPU clusters for the Deep level. |

### 3. NAAMII’s duties as a consortium member

| Duty | What NAAMII does |
|---|---|
| Set-up | Sign the partnership agreement (M1–2), recruit staff (M2–4), go through the system audit of partners (M2–6), and help build the shared templates, procurement plan and a detailed plan for each component. |
| Governance | NAAMII’s head sits on the Project Accountability Team, which meets at least twice a year. NAAMII staff join the monthly Project Implementation Team and the semi-annual and annual reviews. |
| Reporting | Monthly and quarterly narrative and financial reports to YI-Lab, input to the EU’s annual interim reports and the final report, and annual external audits. In the last six months there is also an external evaluation and expenditure verification, plus evaluations by Nepal’s Social Welfare Council (the government body that approves the project). |
| Monitoring data | NAAMII is named as a collector of the data for Output 1.1, its digital skills target. Tangible Careers analytics also feed the outcome-level monitoring. |
| EU visibility | Curricula, modules, platforms and certificates must credit EU funding in both English and Nepali, and events must carry EU branding. That covers the AI School and Tangible Careers themselves. |

### 4. What the EU will expect to see

| Indicator | Target |
|---|---|
| Youth completing Foundation and Deep AI courses | 1,500 Foundation and 500 Deep, per the activity text (the binding figure is in Annex C) |
| Youth completing internships and fellowships | 800 |
| Learners with improved skill scores on standardised tests | 80% |
| Trained youth in a job, internship or self-employment within 6 months | 40% |
| Hackathon solutions developed (co-delivered) | 9 |

The application also promises 30% of participants in permanent jobs or their own businesses during the project. It relies on NAAMII’s track record: it argues the Outcome 1 targets are realistic because AI School alumni went on to AI jobs, PhDs and Master’s degrees, and promotions. On sustainability, it commits that:

- the AI School stays available after the project;
- Tangible Careers serves as a foundation for a national digital skills strategy, with skills frameworks aligned to international standards;
- NAAMII builds lasting expertise in youth engagement in AI.
