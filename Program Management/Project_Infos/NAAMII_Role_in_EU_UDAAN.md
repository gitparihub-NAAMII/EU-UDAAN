## Slide 7

- Standards to Deliver
- What every NAAMII activity in UDAAN has to meet
- Women and inclusion
- At least 40% women, priority for marginalised groups, and competitive selection for the advanced level and for fellowships
- Placement matching
- Based on verified skills, not academic certificates
- Teaching approach
- In-person and online mix, content adapted to Nepal, short repeated sessions, offline kits as backup when connectivity fails
- Guiding principles
- Human-rights-based approach (all five principles) and gender equality and social inclusion; environmental impact tracked with a “Greening Matrix”
- Data
- Results broken down by sex, age, disability, rural/urban location and ethnic group; backed by competency rubrics, portfolios and certificates
- Required equipment
- High-performance computers, licensed software, reliable internet, and GPU clusters for the Deep level

_(6 images on this slide)_

> **Notes:** Section 2 of the source: standards NAAMII must deliver to.
