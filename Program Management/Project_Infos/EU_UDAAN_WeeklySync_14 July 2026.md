## Slide 4 – Project Roadmap

_(The roadmap itself is a picture on the slide.)_ The speaker notes define the nine partnership sectors:

| Sector | Who belongs here | Examples | Main purpose in UDAAN |
|---|---|---|---|
| Education | Colleges, universities, campuses, academic institutions that directly help reach students | TU-affiliated, private and community colleges, universities, management/IT/social science colleges | Student mobilization, cohort delivery, faculty coordination, venue/lab support |
| Municipality | Local government at city, municipality, rural municipality or ward level | Metropolitan cities, municipalities, rural municipalities, ward offices | Local youth outreach, community mobilization, local rollout, civic innovation linkage |
| Science & Engineering | Technical, STEM, research, engineering and innovation-focused institutions | Engineering colleges, technical institutes, research labs, robotics clubs, STEM associations, innovation labs | Technical mentors, applied AI problems, deep skill support, hackathon/project support |
| Government Organizations | Formal national or provincial public institutions, excluding municipalities | Ministries, departments, provincial ministries, government training centers, national boards, authorities | Institutional endorsement, policy alignment, public-sector pathways, scale support |
| Small Enterprises & Businesses | Local businesses, SMEs, cooperatives, startups and business networks | SMEs, cooperatives, local enterprises, chambers, startup groups, women-led businesses | Real-world business problems, entrepreneurship exposure, local AI adoption |
| Fintech | Banking, digital finance, insurance, payment and fintech partners | Banks, digital wallets, microfinance institutions, insurance companies, fintech startups | Digital economy exposure, finance-related AI use cases, industry mentorship |
| Policy Makers / MPs | Individuals or groups influencing policy, legislation or public discourse | MPs, policy advisors, parliamentary committees, think tanks, policy associations | Policy dialogue, advocacy, visibility, system-level influence |
| Hospitality | Tourism, hotel, travel, food service and service-sector partners | Hotels, restaurants, travel companies, tourism associations, hospitality training institutes | Service-sector AI use cases, employment exposure, customer-service innovation |
| Health | Healthcare, public health, hospitals, clinics, health-tech and medical institutions | Hospitals, clinics, health NGOs, public health bodies, med-tech/health-tech groups | Health AI use cases, applied problem statements, public health innovation |
