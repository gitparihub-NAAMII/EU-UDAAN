## Slide 19

- Risks & Contingency
- Key risks by component and how the action will respond

| Component | Key risks | Contingency |
|---|---|---|
| Pathshala | Poor internet and power; monsoon, floods & landslides; gender and social barriers | Low-bandwidth, offline Shikshya.org; USB training kits; flexible scheduling; targeted outreach for ≥40% women |
| Karyashala | Limited incubation space; elections & unrest; scarce investment; travel barriers for rural youth | Hybrid/virtual events; calendar aligned to elections; links to co-ops, banks, accelerators; travel subsidies & regional events |
| Karmashala | Mentor–mentee distance; change in municipal leadership; low job absorption; unequal access | MoUs with municipalities; wider pool of host organisations; remote-area outreach; digital mentoring tools |
| Nitishala | No permanent hub space; weak municipal ownership and budgets; resistance to youth participation | Space via colleges, co-ops, FNCCI & municipal facilities; push for municipal budget lines; joint implementation |
| Cross-cutting | Political unrest; exchange-rate swings or late disbursement; pandemics and disasters; visibility non-compliance | Contingency calendar; quarterly financial reviews & reserves; shift online; dedicated comms officer, designer & video editor |

> **Notes:** Section 2.1.4(ii), pp.27–28: risk analysis and contingency plan across physical, environmental, political, economic and social risks for each component and for cross-cutting project management, EU visibility and financial oversight.
