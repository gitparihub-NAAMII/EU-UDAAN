## 3. AI Native — 2026 target check

| Month end | Aug | Sep | Oct | Nov | Dec |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plan (14 July weekly sync) | 455 | 819 | 1,183 | 1,546 | 2,000 |
| Actual / tentative | 280 | 451 | 531 | **≈751** | **≈1,191** |

The schedule above reaches about **60% of the 2,000 goal**. Closing the remaining ≈810 needs at least one of:
- **Bigger online cohorts:** 150 instead of 60 in N19, N23 and N28 adds ≈270.
- **A second session on college days:** a morning and an afternoon group at ISMT, Uniglobe and Global adds ≈120.
- **Weekly open cohorts** from 16 Nov (6 more × 40) adds ≈240.
- **Self-paced AI Native on Shikshya.org,** counted once learners claim the skill on Tangible Careers, covers the rest.

If none of these is possible, report a revised 2026 figure (≈1,200) to the PIT and PAT in the November report.
