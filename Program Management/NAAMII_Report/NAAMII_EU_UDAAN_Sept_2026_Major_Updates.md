1. NAAMII delivered six AI Native cohorts in September, reaching 171 participants across Khwopa College of Engineering, NAAMII staff, Municipality Fellows, Sunway College (including an open cohort on 28 September) and MIT College Nepal, bringing the total trained since July to 451.

Each cohort introduced AI Nativity, the AI Skills Pyramid and responsible use of AI through demonstrations and practical exercises tailored to the group. Event reports record active participation and strong interest in applying AI to study, municipal work, documentation and programme tasks.

### 2. Tangible Careers release notes

Early September cohorts surfaced challenges with technical issues on the platform, claiming skills and support for non-technical learners. NAAMII responded with proactive communication, workarounds, evidence samples, hands-on guidance and automated responses. In parallel, NAAMII worked with Tangible to resolve these issues, and the following updates were released:

| Area | Update | Benefit for UDAAN |
|---|---|---|
| Certificates | Support for multiple partners: up to 10 partner logos per certificate, in any order | Consortium and co-hosting partner logos can appear together, supporting EU visibility |
| Certificates | New certificate design with a cleaner banner and signature section | More professional certificates for learners |
| Certificates | Fixed an upload issue where some cropped logo images failed due to size limits | Partner logos upload reliably |
| Evaluators | Evaluators can accept all AI-suggested grades for a skill at once | Faster review of learner evidence and verification of competency |
| General | Clearer, more accurate error messages across the app | Learners and staff see the real reason for a failure, reducing support requests |

Certificate issuance and skill-map customisation remain on the October task list.

### 3. AI Foundation trainer recruitment

NAAMII is onboarding AI Foundation trainers. The call received 7 applicants (5 for AI Foundation only and 2 who also applied for AI Native), and candidates were shortlisted in September. Onboarding and orientation are underway ahead of AI Foundation delivery.
