## Progress during the reporting period (highlights)

1. NAAMII scaled AI Native delivery in September, documenting seven cohorts (fifth, sixth, seventh, eighth, ninth, tenth and an open cohort) at Khwopa College of Engineering, NAAMII, the Municipality Fellows Residential Program, Sunway College and MIT College Nepal, reaching 186 participants during the month.

## Detailed program progress as per workplan

### Output 1.1: Digital skills and knowledge strengthened among young women and men, entrepreneurs, government officials, and CSO/CBOs

#### Activity 1.1.1: AI School Offering Tiered Hybrid Courses

During September 2026, NAAMII moved AI School implementation from mobilisation into regular cohort delivery. AI Native remained the immediate delivery priority and was delivered across academic, institutional and municipal audiences. Supporting work covered trainer shortlisting, Tangible skill mapping, website launch and preparation of online delivery modality. AI Foundation and AI Deep remained at the preparation stage.

Detailed activities and progress:

- **Fifth cohort, Khwopa College of Engineering.** Delivered on 2 September 2026 in Bhaktapur to 50 participants, a mixed group of engineering students, business professionals, radiologists, and college staff. The session introduced AI Nativity and the AI Skills Pyramid and explored AI applications in engineering, business, radiology-related work, and institutional administration. The diverse composition enriched discussion, and participants engaged actively with application-focused questions, laying out a foundation for continued collaboration with the college.
- **Sixth cohort, NAAMII staff.** Delivered on 7 September 2026 to 15 NAAMII staff, fulfilling the staff AI Native session announced in August. The session linked AI Native work practices to drafting, summarizing, documentation, coordination and programme support, strengthening internal readiness for responsible AI use within NAAMII's own operations.
- **Seventh cohort, Municipality Fellows (Fellows Residential Program).** Delivered on 9 September 2026 at Hotel Maya Manor Boutique, Hattisar, Kathmandu, as NAAMII's session within the five-day UDAAN residential training. Eleven fellows from various municipalities took part, alongside staff of Heifer Nepal and Youth Innovation Lab. Participants explored AI support for official communication, reporting, planning and citizen-service tasks, completing the residential-session commitment carried over from August.
- **Eighth Cohort, Sunway College.** Delivered on 10 September 2026 to 35 Sunway College students, focusing on AI use in academic learning, writing, research, presentations and problem-solving. Students showed strong interest and connected AI concepts with real academic use cases.
- **Ninth Cohort, Sunway College.** Delivered on 28 September 2026 to 15 undergraduate students at Sunway College, Kathmandu, as a second AI Native session at the college during the month. The session covered AI Native work practices and the AI Skills Pyramid, helping students apply AI to academic learning, writing and research.
- **Tenth cohort, MIT College Nepal.** Delivered on 24 September 2026 to 40 students at MIT College Nepal, covering AI Native work practices, the AI Skills Pyramid and responsible use through demonstrations and practical examples.
- **Eleventh cohort, Man Mohan Memorial Institute of Health sciences (MMIHS).** Delivered on 08 October 2026 to 60 students at MMIHS Nepal, covering AI Native work practices, the AI Skills Pyramid and responsible use through demonstrations and practical examples.
- **Additional scheduled cohorts.** The PIT cohort calendar also scheduled an NSSR cohort (17 September); its attendance and event records are being consolidated and will be reflected in cumulative figures once verified.
- **Trainer recruitment and delivery of readiness.** The trainer call closed with 11 AI Native trainer applicants and 7 AI Foundation trainer applicants (including 2 who applied for both roles). Applicants were shortlisted during September, and onboarding and orientation to the AI Skills Pyramid and problem-based delivery are being finalised.
- **Tangible skills, certification, and course content.** AI Native skills were mapped and published, and a learner community is being configured with access and settings. The Tangible certificate and the customisation of the skill map remain under development by the tech group and are expected to be completed before Chhath, within October and the first week of November 2026. AI Native course videos are being prepared to upload to Sikshya.org.
- **Online delivery artifacts for learners outside the Valley.** NAAMII is developing an online delivery artifact so that AI Native sessions can be conducted remotely for learners outside the Kathmandu Valley. This will extend AI School reach beyond in-person venues and is expected to be completed before November 2026.
- **AI School website.** The UDAAN website, presenting the three AI School pathways and learner registration, went live during September, completing the landing-page commitment from August. Content and CMS refinements are continuing.

### Output 2.3: Opportunities for professional experience created for young women and men

#### Activity 2.3.1: Internships and fellowships in the private sector

Work under the internships and fellowships activity remained preparatory. During September, Karmashala advanced to the proposal stage, and the Nepal technology-company pool was expanded from 60 to 64 companies to support the company/problem-library approach.

**Key progress:**

- Karmashala concept and plan were presented at weekly PIT meetings and advanced into a formal proposal linking company problems, AI Foundation training, learner portfolios and verified competency.
- The company pool was expanded to 64 companies, from which the initial group of approachable companies and focal contacts is being validated.
- The learner pipeline was strengthened through the Tangible learner community, which will support future opportunity matching once verified profiles are in place.
- No internship or fellowship placements were initiated during September; company outreach, problem validation and partner commitment remain on track ahead of the planned December 2026 start.

## Risk levels

_Any changes in risk levels (likelihood and/or impact) and, if so, the mitigations required? Any new risks?_

- No major change in the overall risk level was identified. Learner onboarding challenges, including technical issues, claiming skills on Tangible and support for non-technical learners, were mitigated through proactive communication, evidence samples, hands-on guidance and automated responses.
- The pending Tangible certificate creates a minor timing dependency for certification reporting; this is mitigated by the tech group's committed completion before Chhath and by continued evidence collection in the meantime.
- Reliance on in-person venues limited reach beyond the Kathmandu Valley; the online delivery artifact under development addresses this.
