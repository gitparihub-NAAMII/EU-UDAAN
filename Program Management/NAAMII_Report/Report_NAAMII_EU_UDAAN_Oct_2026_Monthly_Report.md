## Progress during the reporting period (highlights)

1. NAAMII delivered two AI Native cohorts in October, reaching 80 participants at Manmohan Institute of Health Science (8 October) and Lord Buddha Education Foundation (LBEF), Maitidevi (12 October), bringing the total trained since July to 531.
2. NAAMII is in talks with the industry department at Singha Durbar.
3. An MoU between NAAMII and Budhanilkantha Municipality is in progress.
4. NAAMII has opened a call for an open cohort and will deliver pilot training to an online cohort, ISMT College, Uniglobe College and Global College. Training for MBL Bank and NEA is being planned.
5. The AI Native concept note and detailed training document (objectives, course structure, delivery methodology, duration and certification process) were shared with a college partner on 29 October to support further collaboration.
6. Sumi Dhakal joined the UDAAN team as Project Manager in October, and weekly PIT and UDAAN Platform meetings continued.

## Detailed program progress as per workplan

### Output 1.1: Digital skills and knowledge strengthened among young women and men, entrepreneurs, government officials, and CSO/CBOs

#### Activity 1.1.1: AI School Offering Tiered Hybrid Courses

During October 2026, NAAMII continued regular AI Native cohort delivery, with two cohorts reaching 80 participants (531 trained in total since July). In parallel, NAAMII progressed partnership discussions with the industry department at Singha Durbar and an MoU with Budhanilkantha Municipality, and prepared the next round of trainings with colleges, an online cohort and public and private sector institutions. AI Foundation and AI Deep remained at the preparation stage.

Detailed activities and progress:

- **Eleventh cohort, Manmohan Institute of Health Science.** Delivered on 8 October 2026 at Soalteemode to 42 participants, covering AI Native work practices, the AI Skills Pyramid and responsible use through demonstrations and practical examples.
- **Twelfth cohort, Lord Buddha Education Foundation (LBEF).** Delivered on 12 October 2026 at LBEF, Maitidevi to 38 participants.
- **Open cohort.** NAAMII opened a call for an open cohort for the public; onboarding of participants is under way.
- **Online cohort.** A pilot training will be delivered to an online cohort, extending AI School reach to learners beyond in-person venues in the Kathmandu Valley.
- **College trainings.** Trainings are to be delivered at ISMT College, Uniglobe College and Global College.
- **Institutional trainings.** Training for MBL Bank (private sector) and NEA (public sector) is being planned.
- **Trainer recruitment.** Applications for AI Native and AI Foundation trainer roles continued to be screened. [7 shortlisted]
- Tangible certificate and skill-map customization, after holidays.
- Online delivery artifact, also after holidays (Nov 30).
