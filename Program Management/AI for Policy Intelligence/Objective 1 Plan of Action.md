# AI for Policy Intelligence – Objective 1 Plan of Action

> **Draft, under construction.** This plan is still being worked on. Timings, cohort sizes and anything marked *proposed* are not agreed yet and will change.

US Embassy · AI Elevate Nepal · NAAMII · status as of 24 Nov 2026

## Objective 1 at a glance

Objective 1, AI for Policy Intelligence, trains at least 75 people in two cohorts to use AI tools for regulatory analysis, and each participant or team produces a Regulatory Bottleneck Dossier. NAAMII leads it inside the AI Elevate Nepal consortium (All In Foundation, IDI, NAAMII, Areta Public Relations). Source: the consortium proposal (internal document, not published here).

| Commitment | Target | Verified by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| People trained | 75 across 2 cohorts | Attendance and completion records |
| Skills applied | At least 80% use AI tools to identify and document one or more regulatory bottlenecks within 3 months of training | Dossiers, technical review log, 3-month follow-up survey |
| Quality | A sample of dossiers validated by technical review and stakeholder interviews | Technical review log |

**Who takes part:** entrepreneurs and founders (technology, digital services, innovation-driven firms, firms in or seeking U.S.-Nepal trade and investment), business leaders, government officials (trade, investment, industry, finance, taxation, company registration, digital economy, innovation), and communications, public affairs, investor-relations and regulatory-affairs staff.

**Why the timing matters:** the dossiers are the evidence base for the rest of the project. Objective 3 working groups organise around them, Objective 4 picks its priority barriers from them, and Objective 5 stores the verified set in the shared repository.

The proposal fixes the targets, activities and month-level schedule. Week-level timing, cohort sizes and anything marked *proposed* below are suggestions where the proposal is silent.

## Deliverables

The proposal names five deliverables for Objective 1; the review log is added because the M&E plan uses it as evidence.

| Deliverable | Due | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Participant learning materials and templates | End of M1 | Session plans, dossier kit and tool guides are approved and branded |
| Regulatory Bottleneck Dossiers | End of M2 (Cohort 1), end of M3 (Cohort 2) | One per participant or team, with all template fields, a source log and an AI-use statement |
| AI-assisted policy analysis | With each dossier | The analysis shows where AI assisted and where human judgment was applied |
| Training completion certificates | At each capstone | Issued to participants who meet attendance and submit a dossier (*proposed* rule) |
| Final workshop proceedings | M4 | Both capstones documented, with the bottleneck synthesis |
| Technical review log | End of M3 | Every dossier reviewed; the sample validated by stakeholder interviews |

Each dossier covers the same eight points: the affected business process, the responsible agency or agencies, the current rule or process, evidence of the bottleneck, affected stakeholders, business or investment impact, source links, and an initial reform or communications recommendation.

*Proposed* working measures to track weekly during delivery: enrolment against target, attendance per session, dossier drafts and finals submitted, and dossiers passing review.

## Risks and mitigation

The tightest constraint is time: the proposal gives each cohort one month for the workshop, four sessions, the dossier and the capstone. Likelihood ratings are the proposal's where it gives one.

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation in Objective 1 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One month per cohort is too short for a sound dossier | Not rated in the proposal | Finish all design in M1; fix a weekly rhythm; scope each dossier to one business process; keep office hours open after the capstone |
| Low engagement or drop-out | Medium | Organisational nominations, over-enrolment, weekly reminders, mentoring, certificate tied to dossier submission |
| Government officials cannot commit the time | Medium | Engage ministries and Investment Board Nepal in M1, share dates early, use IDI and Embassy relationships, offer recordings |
| In-person workshops disrupted by strikes, weather or health events | Medium | Hybrid or fully virtual fallback on Zoom |
| Weak digital literacy or internet access | Assumption in the M&E plan | Tool setup at the inception workshop, recordings, low-bandwidth materials, one-to-one help in office hours |
| Improper AI use or unverified outputs | Low | Responsible-AI rules from Session 1, source logs, AI-use disclosure, human verification, review checklist |
| U.S. guest speakers unavailable or time zones clash | Not rated in the proposal | Book in M1, hold evening Nepal-time slots, keep a recorded backup |
| Delays between consortium partners | Low | Monthly coordination meeting, milestone tracking, risk register |
