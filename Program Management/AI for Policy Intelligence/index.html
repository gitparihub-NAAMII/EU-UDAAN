AI for Policy Intelligence

The project page for AI for Policy Intelligence. Its tracker lives in the NAAMII programme dashboard and is kept separate from EU UDAAN.

Open the project tracker All programmes

What the tracker holds

Eight sheets, each added to and edited directly in the dashboard, with an overview that sums them up.

  • TeamWho is on the project and what each person owns
  • GoalsProgress and status against each goal
  • Weekly planThis week's tasks, by owner
  • TargetsTargets and actuals per person
  • To-doOpen tasks, priority and due date
  • DeliverablesWhat is due, and when
  • ChallengesOpen issues and their mitigation
  • Risks & decisionsRisks, decisions and follow-ups

Separate from UDAAN

Nothing recorded here is counted in the EU UDAAN people-trained figures or targets.

Who can open it

The tracker opens after signing in to the dashboard with the Program Management owner login.

Where it sits

In the dashboard under Program Management, as the card beside UDAAN.
