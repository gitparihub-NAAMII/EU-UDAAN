# Foundation Training: Combined Plan

Curriculum (60 hours) plus capacity and cohort plan for 1,500 participants over 24 months.

## 1. Project Parameters

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Total participants (target) | 1,500 |
| Project duration | 24 months (~104 calendar weeks) |
| Trainers | 2 |
| Trainer capacity | 10 hrs/week each (20 trainer-hrs/week combined) |
| Learner total hours | 60 hrs (40 self-study / 20 workshop) |
| Goal | Fewest possible cohorts, minimal continuous teaching |

## 2. Curriculum: Suggested 60-Hour Allocation

Revised allocation: 40 h self-study + 20 h workshop = 60-hour course target.

| Stage | Self study (h) | Workshop (h) | Total (h) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Understanding Agriculture/AI | 6 | 2 | 8 |
| 2. U0 Problem Formulation | 4 | 2 | 6 |
| 3. Infrastructure | 2 | 2 | 4 |
| 4. U1 Descriptive Analytics | 5 | 2 | 7 |
| 5. U2 Diagnostic Analytics | 5 | 2 | 7 |
| 6. U3 Predictive Analytics | 5 | 2 | 7 |
| 7. RAG, Local LLM and Agent Basics | 6 | 2 | 8 |
| 8. Integration, Markdown-driven Workflow and Deployment | 5 | 2 | 7 |
| 9. Capstone and Evaluation | 2 | 4 | 6 |
| **Total** | **40** | **20** | **60** |

U3 can be replaced with additional U1/U2 investigation and agentic workflow work if suitable historical data is unavailable.

**Changes from the first draft**
- The first draft summed to 46 hours, not 60 (26 self-study + 20 workshop). Self-study was raised to 40 hours.
- Agentic AI and the Markdown-driven workflow were folded into stages 7 and 8.
- The capstone workshop was raised from 2 to 4 hours, taking 1 hour each from stages 7 and 8.
- Workshop hours stay at 20, so trainer load is unchanged.
- Evaluation tip: run it in groups of 3-4 (20-25 minutes each), or two parallel sessions with one trainer each.

## 3. Capacity Calculation

- Calendar weeks: 24 months ≈ 104 weeks
- Less setup, festivals (Dashain/Tihar), exams, final evaluation: ~24 weeks
- Usable delivery weeks: **~80**
- Trainer-hours available: 80 weeks × 20 hrs = **~1,600 trainer-hours**
- Only workshop hours use trainer time. Self-study hours do not.

## 4. Cohort Size vs. Trainer Load (20 workshop hrs per participant)

| Cohort size | Cohorts | Trainer-hours needed | Capacity used | Workshop weeks per trainer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20 | 75 | 1,500 | 94% | ~75 (nearly nonstop) |
| 30 | 50 | 1,000 | 63% | ~50 |
| 40 | 38 | 760 | 48% | ~38 |
| 50 | 30 | 600 | 38% | ~30 |

- **At 20 per cohort**, you would use 94% of capacity with no room for dropouts, illness, festivals or reruns.
- **At 40 to 50 per cohort**, workshop delivery shrinks to 30-38 weeks per trainer, so it can run in waves with breaks.
